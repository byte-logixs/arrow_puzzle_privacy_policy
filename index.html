How to play

Easy to learn. Bigger boards and twistier arrows keep it challenging.

1

Tap an arrow

It flies off the board in the direction it points.

2

Find a clear path

An arrow only escapes if nothing is in its way. Hit another arrow and you lose a heart.

3

Clear the board

Send every arrow away to win the level and earn stars.
