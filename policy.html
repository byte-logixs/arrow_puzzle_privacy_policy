Arrow Puzzle · Byte Logixs

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information Arrow Puzzle collects when you play it, why, and the choices you have. It applies to Arrow Puzzle as distributed on Google Play (package com.bytelogixs.arrowpuzzle) and to the web version of the game. Arrow Puzzle is made by Byte Logixs ("we", "us").

  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your progress, coins and settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The game is free and shows ads from Google: a banner, an ad card on the home screen and an occasional ad between levels. You can also choose to watch a video for extra lives, hints or coins.
  • Google collects device information to show these ads, as described below. You can turn off all ads except the ones you choose to watch with the Remove Ads purchase.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

Arrow Puzzle saves your level, lives, hints, coins, stars, badges, daily streak, settings and a record of the purchases you made in the app's private storage on your device (or, on the web, in your browser's local storage). This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device's Settings, by uninstalling the app, or on the web by clearing the site's data in your browser.

2. Ads

Arrow Puzzle shows these ads:

  • a banner along the bottom of the screen;
  • a native ad card on the home screen;
  • a full-screen ad every few levels, between levels;
  • rewarded videos that play only when you tap a Watch ad button for an extra life, a hint, doubled coins or the free gift. The reward is given only if the video was watched.

In the app these ads are provided by Google AdMob, and on the web by Google AdSense, both advertising services of Google. To choose, show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's ads software collects and processes information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the Advertising ID);
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (such as country or city);
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system version, language, app version and network type;
  • how you interact with ads, such as whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped;
  • crash and performance information about the ads software;
  • on the web, cookies, which let Google and its partners show ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your advertising identifier or any other personal information from these ads. We only see combined reports, such as how many ads were shown. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your consent and choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, Arrow Puzzle shows Google's consent form. You can accept or decline personalised ads, and change your choice later from Rewards → Shop → Privacy & ads in the app.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu name varies by device).
  • Personalised ads: you can also manage them at Google Ad Settings, and on the web opt out of third-party cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Rewarded videos: they are always optional. No video plays unless you tap Watch ad.
  • Remove Ads: the optional Remove Ads purchase (or the Starter Pack) turns off the banner, the home screen ad and the ads between levels.

3. In-app purchases

You can buy hints, coins, unlimited lives for a time, the Starter Pack or Remove Ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's payments privacy notice. We never see your name, card or payment details. The app receives only what it needs to deliver your purchase, such as the product and an order identifier, and keeps that on your device so a purchase is not given twice. Purchases are not available in the web version.

5. Children

Arrow Puzzle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share information about you with anyone, except that Google receives the information described in section 2 when the game requests an ad, and Google Play processes your purchases as described in section 3.

7. Security and retention

Ad and purchase requests are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The progress saved on your device stays there until you clear it or remove the app. Google keeps advertising and payment data according to its own retention policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or limit how it is used. Because we don't hold personal information about you, most of these requests are handled by the controls above or by Google's privacy tools. You can also contact us with any question.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play listing.
